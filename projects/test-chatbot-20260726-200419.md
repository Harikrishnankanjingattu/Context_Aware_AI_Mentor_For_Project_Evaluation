### Medical College AI Chatbot

The Medical College AI Chatbot is a conversational interface designed to assist students, faculty, and staff of a medical college with frequently asked questions, academic information, and college resources. This chatbot aims to improve the overall student experience by providing quick and easy access to essential information, reducing the workload on administrative staff, and enhancing the college's online presence.

## 2. Objectives & Scope
### Project Goals

* Develop a conversational AI chatbot that can understand and respond to medical college-related queries.
* Integrate with the college's existing website and student information system (SIS).
* Provide basic information on academic programs, course schedules, and college policies.
* Offer support for students with questions about academic advising, financial aid, and campus life.
* Develop a user-friendly interface for administrators to manage and update chatbot responses.
